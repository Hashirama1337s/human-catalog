# Changelog

## 2.2 (2026)
Shaped by an independent review panel (a historian, a teacher with an accessibility specialist, and a research-data reviewer).
- **People:** 24,057 people from Wikidata with real dates (precision kept, Julian dates corrected), search and "alive in"
  any year. No living people. Notability scales with era so the ancient world is not crowded out; the known bias
  (14% women, leaning to Europe and North America) is stated on the page.
- **Unsung heroes:** 96 contributions whose credit was missed, delayed or given to others, each documented by its source.
- **New parts:** Congo Free State, Bengal famine 1943, Holodomor, Nanjing, Cambodia, Srebrenica; Ibn Khaldun, Zhu Xi,
  Adi Shankara, Guru Nanak; open questions on the Great Divergence, the Americas in 1491, Meroitic, and African iron.
- **Framing:** Columbus, Nzinga, the Holocaust, Partition, the 1833 Abolition Act and Potosí reworded with the facts the
  review found missing. Records of mass violence and enslavement carry a sober notice and are kept out of the games.
  Era names no longer apply European labels to the whole world. "Fitment" is now "Place".
- **Honesty:** the referee claim now says exactly what was checked. All 110 "first/oldest" claims re-checked: 96 confirmed
  word for word in their sources, 14 reworded. 17 date ranges stored consistently.
- **Design:** pastel colours for the 13 systems, an era colour journey, stronger type; all colour pairs pass WCAG AA.
- **Accessibility:** no sideways scrolling on phones, a status line instead of a live tree, keyboard-reachable question
  marks, a text list of migration routes, slider values announced, expanded state on part rows.
- **Pinned lists** are kept between visits and can be shared as a link.

## 2.1 (2026)
- New game: **Which came first?** Two parts, tap the older one. Both games now sit under one **Play** button.
- New: **Solved ledger** on the Unknowns board. When a source settles an open question, its ticket is stamped SHIPPED
  and the answer becomes a real part.
- Fixed: on the public site, closed panels (Unknowns, Play) could show before being opened.
- Phone layout: search takes a full row; question marks on the ruler no longer overlap.
- Archived on Zenodo: doi:10.5281/zenodo.23977246 (all versions), v2.1 = doi:10.5281/zenodo.23977247.

## 2.0 — Second Edition (2026)
- Permanent part numbers stored in the data; one file per part.
- New: Cross-section (everything happening at a date you type), idea threads, "superseded by" links, referee stamp on
  every part, pinned part lists, index of people. Fonts are bundled; the page makes no third-party requests.
- Data: two duplicate Pacific settlement parts merged; place names normalised.
- New: Unknowns board (42 open questions as back-ordered parts, pick-a-theory) and a Guess-the-year game.
- Content: 66 new parts filling measured gaps (Africa 1500-1950, food and migration after 1500, belief and writing after 1900, Southeast Asia, Oceania, women, and the Atlantic, trans-Saharan and Indian Ocean slave trades).
- Referee pass on all new parts and on every established part carrying a "first/oldest/only" word: 62 corrected, none removed.

## 1.0 — First Edition (1 November 2026)
- 669 parts from 300,000 years ago to 2026 across 13 systems; 29 migration routes.
- Every part re-checked against its source by an independent referee desk before release: 173 corrected (wording,
  places, confidence marks; 12 had a date corrected or refined), none removed.
