How this catalog began

We kept running into the same surprise. Working across many different fields, we would find an idea in one place and then meet it again somewhere else, thousands of kilometres away, with no contact between the two. Farming began on several continents on its own. Writing was invented more than once. Some discoveries only made sense when set side by side with another from a different century or a different part of the world.

History books usually tell these stories one country or one subject at a time, so the connections get lost between the chapters. We wanted one place where you could see how everything fits together for humans, on a single timeline you can zoom into with simple clicks, the way a car-parts catalog lets you drill from make to model to the exact part. So we built it, checked every part against a source we opened, and are giving it away. If you spot a mistake or a missing part, tell us. It is your history too.

— Moki & Julio (AI-assisted)

How dates are given

BCE and CE years; no year zero. Deep time is given in years ago, counted from 1950 as archaeologists do. "c." means circa. Ranges are given where specialists give ranges.

Marks

Established broad agreement among specialists. Debated the date or the claim is contested. Interchange the same part was invented independently somewhere else.

Part numbers

HC · system · date code · serial. Date codes: KA = thousands of years ago, B = BCE year, C = CE year. Fiche coordinates (row letter = era, column = span) locate a part on the sheet.

Compiled by

Moki & Julio (AI-assisted). Research desks A (deep time), B (ancient and medieval) and C (modern), and a concept desk. Every part carries the source its researcher opened to check the date. Before publication, every part was re-checked against its source by an independent referee desk; 173 parts were corrected for wording, places and confidence marks, and 12 of those had a date corrected or refined. For the Second Edition, 66 new parts and every established part using a word like "first" or "oldest" were checked again; 62 were corrected.

Selection and gaps

A part is listed when it is the earliest known appearance of something that changed what people could do or think. "Earliest known" means the oldest evidence found so far, not proof that nothing came before. This first edition is a starting set, not a complete record; errors reported are corrected in the next edition.
